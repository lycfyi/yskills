# Cold-read exam B (check set)

Each item gives who sees it, where they are and what they want to do, and the text on screen. Judge every item with your method. The items are in random order.

The items come from five fictional products: a team expense tool, an online course platform (a home-baking course), a coworking desk-booking app, a print-on-demand shop, and a recipe-box subscription.
